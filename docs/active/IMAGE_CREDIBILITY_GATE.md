# Image credibility gate

Any Lumivey production build that contains real or generated people must pass this gate before being accepted.

- Human anatomy and spatial continuity must be physically believable.
- No floating hands, arms, heads or disconnected body fragments.
- No AI reconstruction of a body part when a suitable supplied real image can be faithfully cropped or reused.
- Prefer less visual content over invented anatomy.
- Keep real people recognizable; do not alter face, apparent age or identity.
- Prominent crops must not unintentionally cut off head or face.
- This is a hard quality gate for Preview and v0 output, not a cosmetic preference.

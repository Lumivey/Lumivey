# Adrie / AssetPouwer — v0 handoff

Status: approved for production test after Preview PASS >85%.

## Design authority
The latest approved Adrie Preview is the visual and emotional authority for the build. v0 must reproduce its calm, senior, technical and human character as closely as practical, rather than redesigning AssetPouwer from scratch.

## Preserve
- Strategy ↔ operation as the central positioning.
- Technical credibility: industry, infrastructure, electrical engineering, industrial automation, measurement & control, tunnel technical installations.
- Adrie's practical roots: outside in the cold, factories started up, plans must work outside.
- Calm observation: first look and understand, then decide.
- One subtle human cue from Sunday-morning nature/photography; this is identity and way of looking, not a service.
- Change-case: impact on people and budgets became visible; phased implementation over multiple years became possible.
- Rich source-backed credibility from the current website: relevant diploma/education/professional context/articles/knowledge where useful, without turning the site into a CV dump.
- Verified contact details only.

## Image rules
- Supplied real Adrie photos are primary production assets and beat AI replacements.
- Use them as a source pool, not a quota.
- Keep Adrie recognizable; do not change face, apparent age or identity.
- Never crop a head/face unintentionally.
- Never generate or show floating hands, arms or disconnected body fragments.
- Every visible human body part must have believable anatomical and spatial continuity.
- If a supplied photo already contains the required scene, crop/reuse it faithfully instead of reconstructing the person or body part with AI.
- When uncertain, show less rather than invent anatomy.
- Camera/photography must not dominate; normally one meaningful homepage moment is enough.

## Homepage calibration
- Professional layer dominant; personal layer subtle.
- Avoid a generic consultant template.
- Avoid monotonous dark/light/dark/light block stacking where an editorial rhythm better matches the Preview.
- The result should still feel specifically like Adrie when company name and profession are hidden.
- The result should still reveal why Adrie is professionally distinctive even if his personal photos are removed.

## Build gate
The real website should target >85% fidelity to the approved Preview on relevant visual, content and emotional characteristics, while preserving richer verified source information in appropriate pages/sections.

Any implausible human image, invented fact, loss of key source-backed meaning, or generic-consultant flattening is a build failure, not a cosmetic issue.

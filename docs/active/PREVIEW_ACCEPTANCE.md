# Lumivey — Preview acceptance

A Preview is not accepted only because it is technically clean or sector-appropriate.

## Required checks

1. Professional anchor
   - The homepage makes the entrepreneur's actual work/domain credible and concrete.

2. Personal recognition anchor
   - The homepage contains at least one evidence-backed personal recognition anchor unless Discovery explicitly rules that out.
   - It may be a person, first name(s), a meaningful object, scene, phrase, story cue or atmosphere.
   - The form must fit this entrepreneur; do not repeat one gimmick across cases.

3. Balance
   - Personal meaning must not erase professional credibility.
   - Professional content must not flatten the entrepreneur into a generic sector template.

4. Evidence discipline
   - Personal anchors must come from confirmed conversation/source evidence.
   - A hobby may shape atmosphere/identity but must not silently become a service.
   - Explicit user boundaries override this default.

5. Source richness
   - Relevant confirmed source-backed facts, especially contact data and distinctive professional context, should not disappear without reason.

6. Recognizability
   - Ask: if company name and profession were covered, would this still feel recognizably like this entrepreneur?

## Gate
A Preview that is polished but generic is WARN/FAIL, not PASS.
